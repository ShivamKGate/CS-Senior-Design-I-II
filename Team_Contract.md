## 1. Project goal

Build a multimodal AI platform that (1) localizes pediatric liver tumor on whole-slide images via a curated Silver/Gold annotation pipeline and pathology segmentation model, and (2) fuses pathology with complementary clinical modalities (radiology, genomics/molecular, outcomes) to support risk stratification, delivered as a research prototype evaluated with pathology stakeholders, not as an FDA-cleared diagnostic device.

## 3. Communication and meeting cadence

- **Team standup:** every 2nd Wednesday from 3-5pm tentatively, async updates in shared chat the same day if someone misses.
- **Advisor meetings:** 2-3 times a week; notes committed to `docs/meetings/` with date, attendees, decisions, and action items.
- **Response expectation:** acknowledge teammate messages within 24 hours on weekdays when possible.
- **Conflict:** raise issues early in standup; if unresolved in one week, escalate to advisor with a short written summary of options.

## 4. Work standards

- No PHI, credentials, or unapproved clinical data will be committed to our GitHub repository or included in code files submitted for the course; any relevant code shared for review will exclude protected patient information.
- Training and evaluation only on approved institutional/hospital environments and datasets.
- Document major design decisions (model choice, fusion approach, eval metrics) in the repo.
- Shared deadline rule: flag blockers at least 48 hours before a graded submission when feasible.

## 5. Contribution and credit

- Both members contribute to coding, writing, and presentations proportional to role; ghosting a deliverable is a contract breach.
- If contribution becomes uneven, document hours/tasks for two weeks, then renegotiate roles with advisor present if needed.
